# Adetiq Ltd — Head of IT

**Period:** May 2016 – June 2019  
**Industry:** Document Management / Legal / Professional Services  
**Location:** UK

## Overview

Owned IT strategy, delivery, and operations across the organisation. Member of the senior management team, responsible for IT, security, compliance, and technical innovation. Led a multidisciplinary team and drove organisation-wide improvement in security posture, process maturity, delivery capability, and commercial technical outcomes.

## Responsibilities

- IT strategy ownership and delivery management
- Security, compliance, audit readiness, and risk management
- Line management and capability development for engineering and IT staff
- Ownership of ISO 27001, ISO 9001, and PCI DSS-related controls and process implementation
- Designing and delivering enterprise systems for global clients
- AI / ML capability development for document-processing services
- Infrastructure management, supplier management, and operational improvement

## Key Achievements

- Reduced known security vulnerabilities by approximately 90% through stronger policy, automation, standardisation, and disciplined engineering practices.
- Led ISO 27001:2013 and ISO 9001:2015 implementation, achieving certification in 2018.
- Worked directly with auditors, senior leadership, and operational teams to define policy, collect evidence, implement controls, and embed more mature governance processes.
- Built an AI / ML solution to automate large-scale document processing, handling more than 12 million documents through OCR and classification and creating new revenue opportunities for the business.
- Designed and delivered multiple enterprise systems used across global clients.
- Reduced operational risk through stronger access controls, better configuration management, and more consistent infrastructure and delivery standards.
- Trained and developed the internal team in the processes, standards, and technical practices needed to operate effectively in a more controlled and audit-ready environment.
