# Family Investments — Technical Architect

**Period:** January 2013 – November 2014  
**Industry:** Financial Services / Mutual / Investment Management  
**Location:** UK

## Overview

Head-hunted to join Family Investments' Technical Strategy department as a senior member of the IS division. Family Investments was a mutual organisation specialising in financial solutions for families, managing more than £4.5 billion for 1.8 million people in the UK at the time. The Technical Strategy function was responsible for investigating, documenting, recommending, and implementing new technology across the organisation.

## Responsibilities

- Technical research, investigation, and recommendation of new technologies
- Architecture and design of internal systems and operational tooling
- Delivery of new software solutions across the organisation
- Modernising service desk tooling and improving operational support processes
- Collaboration with IS, compliance, operations, and business teams
- Translating business and operational problems into practical technical solutions

## Key Achievements

- Designed a new compliance system to digitise documents previously stored on microfiche, improving accessibility, control, and operational efficiency.
- Built a C# WinForms application enabling the service desk to manage Out of Office settings without requiring direct access to user email accounts, reducing support overhead and improving control.
- Created a scheduling application allowing operations teams to configure automated jobs, receive failure notifications, enable automatic retries, and integrate across Family Investments systems.
- Modernised service desk support tooling to improve consistency, support-team autonomy, and operational responsiveness.
- Helped strengthen internal technical capability by introducing more effective and maintainable business tooling in a regulated financial-services environment.
