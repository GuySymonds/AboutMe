# Family Investments — Technical Architect

**Period:** January 2013 – November 2014  
**Industry:** Financial Services / Mutual / Investment Management  
**Location:** UK

## Overview

Head-hunted to join Family Investments' Technical Strategy department as a senior member of the IS division. Family Investments is a mutual organisation specialising in financial solutions for families, managing more than £4.5 billion for 1.8 million people in the UK at the time.

Technical Strategy had the remit for investigating, documenting, and implementing new technology across the organisation.

## Responsibilities

- Technical research, investigation, and recommendation of new technologies
- Architecture and design of internal systems and tooling
- Delivery of new software solutions across the organisation
- Modernising service desk tooling and operations
- Collaboration with IS, compliance, and business teams

## Key Achievements

- Designed a new compliance system to store documents previously held on microfiche, migrating them to a digital platform.
- Built a C# WinForms application enabling the service desk to manage Out of Office settings without requiring direct access to user email accounts.
- Created a new scheduling application allowing operations to configure automated jobs, receive failure notifications, enable automatic retries, and integrate with all Family Investments systems.
- Modernised service desk support tools to improve efficiency and consistency.

## Technologies & Tools

- C# / .NET / WinForms
- MSSQL Server
- Windows Server
- Service desk tooling
- Financial services compliance systems
