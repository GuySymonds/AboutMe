# Southern Water — Digital Lead (Interim)

**Period:** August 2020 – January 2021  
**Industry:** Utilities / Public Sector  
**Location:** UK

## Overview

Brought in as an interim Digital Lead to stabilise digital engineering after years of instability, data loss incidents, and weak architectural controls. The role required rapid diagnosis of the problem areas and execution of improvements while maintaining live services.

## Responsibilities

- Digital delivery leadership across engineering teams
- Repairing and improving delivery processes and engineering standards
- Architecture design and technical decision-making
- Stakeholder engagement across technology and business functions
- Leading delivery of high-visibility public projects

## Key Achievements

- Repaired a failing digital delivery pipeline and aligned the team around clearer engineering practices and standards.
- Led delivery of the Beachbuoy project: a high-visibility public health service used to track beach water quality and spill events, ensuring real-time updates for the public.
- Designed an enterprise-grade authentication microservice, resolving long-standing security and identity gaps across the digital estate.
- Designed a notification microservice to improve alerting and communication across digital services.
- Built multiple mobile application prototypes to validate new service concepts and accelerate stakeholder sign-off.

## Technologies & Tools

- C# / .NET Core / ASP.NET Core
- Azure
- Microservices architecture
- OAuth2 / OpenID Connect / authentication patterns
- REST APIs
- Mobile prototyping
