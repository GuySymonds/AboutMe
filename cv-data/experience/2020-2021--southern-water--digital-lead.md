# Southern Water — Digital Lead (Interim)

**Period:** August 2020 – January 2021  
**Industry:** Utilities / Public Sector  
**Location:** UK

## Overview

Brought in as an interim Digital Lead to stabilise digital engineering after years of instability, data-loss incidents, and weak architectural controls. The role required quickly diagnosing technical and organisational problems, restoring confidence in delivery, and improving engineering standards while maintaining live services.

## Responsibilities

- Digital delivery leadership across engineering teams
- Repairing and improving delivery processes and engineering standards
- Architecture design and technical decision-making
- Stakeholder engagement across technology and business functions
- Leading delivery of high-visibility public digital services
- Shaping practical solutions to long-standing security, identity, and service-design problems

## Key Achievements

- Stabilised a failing digital delivery pipeline and aligned the team around clearer engineering practices, architecture standards, and decision-making.
- Led delivery of the Beachbuoy project, a high-visibility public service used to report beach water quality and spill events more quickly and reliably than the previous process.
- Designed an enterprise-grade authentication microservice to address long-standing security and identity gaps across the digital estate.
- Designed a notification microservice to improve alerting and communication across digital services.
- Built multiple mobile application prototypes to validate service ideas, reduce ambiguity, and accelerate stakeholder sign-off.
- Helped restore confidence in delivery by combining hands-on technical leadership with clearer structure and better engineering discipline.
