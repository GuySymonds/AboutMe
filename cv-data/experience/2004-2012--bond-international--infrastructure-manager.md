# Bond International Software — Infrastructure Manager

**Period:** 2004 – December 2012  
**Industry:** HR Software / Recruitment Technology  
**Location:** UK

## Overview

Joined Bond International Software in 2004 on the IT helpdesk and progressed through increasingly senior support and infrastructure roles to Infrastructure Manager. Bond provided HR and recruitment software to enterprise clients globally. In the final role, acted as senior technical lead for Bond's largest blue-chip and public-sector customers, while also owning significant internal infrastructure improvement work.

## Responsibilities

- Infrastructure design and implementation for enterprise and public-sector clients
- Line management of a Technical Analyst and two Infrastructure Specialists
- Senior technical ownership for complex customer environments
- Resource allocation across multiple parallel delivery and support initiatives
- Penetration testing analysis, remediation planning, and security advisory
- Redesign and rebuild of internal development, training, and QA environments
- Collaboration with delivery, support, and client stakeholders on environment strategy and operational readiness

## Key Achievements

- Designed and implemented enterprise production environments for major clients including Hays, MPI, AstraZeneca, Citi, UBS, and Santander.
- Designed and implemented environments for government and quasi-government organisations including Remploy, Ingeus, and JHP.
- Led internal and external penetration-testing reviews, advising the business on remediation priorities and security improvements.
- Redesigned Bond's internal development, training, and QA infrastructure to improve supportability and delivery effectiveness.
- Progressed from IT helpdesk to Infrastructure Manager over the course of the tenure, building broad credibility across support, infrastructure, security, and client delivery.
