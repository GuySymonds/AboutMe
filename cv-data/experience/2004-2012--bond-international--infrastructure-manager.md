# Bond International Software — Infrastructure Manager

**Period:** 2004 – December 2012  
**Industry:** HR Software / Recruitment Technology  
**Location:** UK

## Overview

Joined Bond International Software in 2004 on the IT helpdesk and progressed through the support ladder to the Infrastructure Manager role. In this role, had a Technical Analyst and two Infrastructure Specialists reporting directly.

Bond International Software provided HR and recruitment software to enterprise clients globally. As Technical Lead for Bond's largest blue-chip and enterprise clients, responsible for designing and implementing production environments and providing senior technical guidance across the client base.

## Responsibilities

- Infrastructure design and implementation for enterprise clients (including Hays, MPI, AstraZeneca, Citi, UBS, Santander)
- Team management: Technical Analyst and two Infrastructure Specialists
- Resource allocation across multiple projects
- Penetration testing analysis and security advisory
- Internal infrastructure redesign and rebuild (development, training, QA environments)
- Designing and implementing environments for government organisations (Remploy, Ingeus, JHP)

## Key Achievements

- Designed and implemented enterprise infrastructure environments for major blue-chip clients including Hays, Citi, UBS, AstraZeneca, and Santander.
- Designed and implemented environments for government quangos including Remploy, Ingeus, and JHP.
- Led internal and external penetration testing and cybersecurity reviews, advising the business on remediation and mitigation strategies.
- Progressed from IT helpdesk to Infrastructure Manager over the course of the tenure.
- Redesigned and rebuilt Bond's internal infrastructure including development, training, and QA environments.
